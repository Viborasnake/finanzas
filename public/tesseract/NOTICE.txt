MisFinanzas uses Tesseract.js and Tesseract trained language data for local OCR.

Tesseract.js: https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js (Apache-2.0)
Tesseract OCR: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract (Apache-2.0)

The worker, OCR core and Spanish/English language files are served by this
application so transaction screenshots do not need to be sent to a third-party
OCR service.
